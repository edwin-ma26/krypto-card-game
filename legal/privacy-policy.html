Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Krypto ("Krypto," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our iOS application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Account information Email address, password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication, never in plain text), display name Creating and securing your account, identifying you to other players
Gameplay & profile data Puzzle solve times, scores, ranked rating, match history, competition results, referral codes Powering ranked matches, leaderboards, competitions, and your player profile
Social data Friends list, friend requests, opponent display names/emails visible to matched players Enabling the friends and multiplayer features
Purchase information Product purchased (e.g., Competition Mode), transaction status Unlocking paid features you purchase; processed by Apple, not stored by us
Device & diagnostic data Basic device/app operation data collected automatically by Firebase infrastructure Keeping the App running reliably and securely

2. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you — when you create an account, set a display name, add friends, or make a purchase.
  • Automatically — gameplay results (solve times, scores, match outcomes) are recorded as you play and stored in our database (Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore).
  • From Apple — in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store and StoreKit; we receive only confirmation that a purchase was completed, not your payment details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, maintain, and secure your account.
  • To operate core features: ranked matches, live multiplayer matches, competitions, and friends.
  • To display your display name, rank, and results to other players you match or compete with.
  • To unlock features you purchase.
  • To maintain, debug, and improve the App.
  • To communicate with you about your account, if necessary (e.g., a required account or security notice).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Sharing of Information

We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Other players — your display name and gameplay results are visible to players you are matched with, competing against, or connected with as friends. Depending on the feature, your email may be visible to friends/opponents within the App.
  • Service providers — we use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions) to store account data and run backend logic, and Apple (StoreKit / App Store) to process in-app purchases. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security commitments.
  • Legal requirements — if required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Krypto, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers — if Krypto is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not otherwise sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and gameplay data for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the App's features (such as ranked history and leaderboards). You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard services (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store and protect your data, including encryption in transit and access-controlled security rules that restrict who can read or write your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access & correction — you can update your email and display name from within the App.
  • Account deletion — you may request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us at the email below. We will delete or anonymize your data except where retention is required by law.
  • Depending on your location (e.g., EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International Users

Krypto's service providers (Google Firebase, Apple) may process and store data in the United States or other countries. By using the App, you consent to this transfer, storage, and processing.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the App. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
